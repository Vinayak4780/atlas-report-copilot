Cognitive Market Research (CMR). Published 2 April 2025. Sector: Energy.

## Executive summary

Electric vehicle adoption is driving investment in public fast chargers, workplace charging and home wallboxes. Software for charge-point management and payment is a growing share of value.

## Scope and methodology

The report covers charging hardware (AC and DC chargers), charge-point management software and installation services, for public and private sites. CMR combined charger deployment databases with vendor interviews.

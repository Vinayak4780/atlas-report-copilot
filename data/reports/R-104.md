# Asia-Pacific Smart Electricity Meter Market 2025

Cognitive Market Research (CMR). Published 14 February 2025. Sector: Utilities. Coverage: Asia-Pacific only.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Executive summary

Asia-Pacific is the world's largest smart electricity meter market. National rollout programmes, prepaid metering and grid-loss reduction targets drive demand. The market is dominated by large state-led tenders.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Scope

The report covers single-phase and three-phase smart electricity meters and their communication modules in Asia-Pacific. Head-end software and installation services are excluded.

## Methodology

CMR modelled installed base and replacement cycles by country, validated with tender awards and interviews with distribution utilities.

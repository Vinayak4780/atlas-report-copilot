# Residential Water Purifier Market 2025

Cognitive Market Research (CMR). Published 22 January 2025. Sector: Consumer Durables. Base year 2024, forecast window 2025-2030.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## Executive summary

Demand for household water purifiers is rising with concerns about water quality, urbanisation and higher disposable incomes. Reverse osmosis (RO) systems dominate in regions with high dissolved solids, while filter pitchers and under-sink filters lead in North America and Europe.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Scope and methodology

The report covers household purifiers: RO, UV, gravity-based and filter systems, including replacement cartridges sold with new units. Commercial and industrial units are excluded. CMR used retail audit data, manufacturer interviews and e-commerce price tracking.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Market size

The global residential water purifier market was valued at USD 28.6 billion in 2024. The figure covers household RO, UV, gravity and filter purifiers and excludes commercial units.

## Forecast

CMR expects the market to grow at a CAGR of 8.3% over the forecast period 2025-2030, supported by first-time adoption in emerging economies and replacement demand elsewhere.

# Smart Water Metering: Industry Outlook 2024

Aquila Insights. Published 18 June 2024. Public excerpt distributed by Cognitive Market Research.

<!-- page: 2 -->
## About Aquila Insights

Aquila Insights is an independent (fictional) research firm focused on metering and utility hardware. This outlook is based on shipment tracking of meter manufacturers and does not cover software or services revenue.

<!-- page: 3 -->
## Executive summary

AMI hardware continues to replace AMR hardware as utilities move to fixed networks. Shipment growth is strongest in Asia-Pacific. Hardware average selling prices continue to decline slowly as ultrasonic designs mature. Headline figures appear in the market data summary on page 14.

<!-- page: 4 -->
## Scope

This outlook measures revenue from smart water meter hardware only: AMR meters, AMI meters and their integrated communication modules. Meter data management software, analytics subscriptions, installation and managed services are excluded. Revenue is measured at manufacturer selling prices.

<!-- page: 5 -->
## Methodology

Aquila tracks quarterly shipments reported by meter manufacturers and multiplies them by estimated average selling prices per region. Forecasts use a shipment-driven model for the window 2024 to 2029.

<!-- page: 6 -->
## Demand drivers

Utilities cite leak detection, billing accuracy and labour savings from remote reads as the main reasons for purchasing smart water meters. Public funding programmes in Asia-Pacific and Europe support large tenders.

<!-- page: 7 -->
## AMR versus AMI

AMR (walk-by or drive-by reading) remains common in cost-sensitive markets. AMI (fixed networks with two-way communication) is preferred for new large-scale deployments because it enables interval data and remote alarms.

<!-- page: 8 -->
## Communication technologies

NB-IoT and LoRaWAN modules are now standard options. Cellular modules raise the bill of materials but avoid the need for a utility-owned network.

<!-- page: 9 -->
## Regional dynamics

Asia-Pacific leads hardware shipments thanks to China and India. Europe shows steady replacement demand. North America shipments are linked to multi-utility AMI programmes.

<!-- page: 10 -->
## Vendor landscape

Hardware supply is concentrated among a small group of meter manufacturers, several of which are also expanding into software. Vendor names are withheld in this public excerpt.

<!-- page: 11 -->
## Pricing trends

Average selling prices for ultrasonic meters continue to fall gradually as volumes rise, while module prices are stable. Price competition is most intense in large Asia-Pacific tenders.

<!-- page: 12 -->
## Risks

Component shortages, delays in public tenders and changes in subsidy programmes are the main risks to the forecast.

<!-- page: 13 -->
## Forecast approach

The forecast covers 2024 to 2029. Aquila does not publish projections beyond 2029 in this outlook.

## Market data summary

Global revenue from smart water meter hardware (AMR and AMI meters including integrated communication modules) reached USD 3.87 billion in 2024. Aquila Insights forecasts a CAGR of 9.8% for 2024-2029. Asia-Pacific accounted for 41% of global hardware revenue in 2024, the largest regional share.

These figures cover hardware only and exclude software and services.
